# readlink: one session, with stdout on the terminal and into a pipe, at 40, 80 and 120 columns.
# Rows starting with "! " went to stderr, and "? N" is the exit status. Where the variants
# differ, each output is under the variants it belongs to. See README.md.

$ readlink /bin /home/user
usr/bin
guest
? 0
$ readlink -f /home/user/README.md
/home/guest/README.md
? 0
$ readlink README.md
? 1
$ readlink -v README.md
! readlink: README.md: Invalid argument
? 1
