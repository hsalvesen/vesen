# truncate: one session, with stdout on the terminal and into a pipe, at 40, 80 and 120 columns.
# Rows starting with "! " went to stderr, and "? N" is the exit status. Where the variants
# differ, each output is under the variants it belongs to. See README.md.

$ truncate -s 0 history.txt
? 0
$ truncate -s 1K blank; truncate -s -24 blank; ls -l history.txt blank
-rw-r--r-- 1 guest guest 1000 Oct  6 20:02 blank
-rw-r--r-- 1 guest guest    0 Oct  6 20:01 history.txt
? 0
$ file blank
blank: data
? 0
$ truncate -s 1G big
! truncate: failed to truncate 'big' at 1073741824 bytes: No space left on device
? 1
$ truncate x
! truncate: you must specify either '--size' or '--reference'
! Try 'truncate --help' for more information.
