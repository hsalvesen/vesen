# chgrp: one session, with stdout on the terminal and into a pipe, at 40, 80 and 120 columns.
# Rows starting with "! " went to stderr, and "? N" is the exit status. Where the variants
# differ, each output is under the variants it belongs to. See README.md.

$ chgrp -v guest README.md
group of 'README.md' retained as guest
? 0
$ chgrp -R guest projects
? 0
$ chgrp has README.md
! chgrp: changing group of 'README.md': Operation not permitted
? 1
$ chgrp bob README.md
! chgrp: invalid group: 'bob'
? 1
